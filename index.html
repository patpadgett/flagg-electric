What areas do you serve?+

Flagg Electric is based in Camdenton, Missouri and serves the entire Lake area. Call with your property's location to confirm service for your project.

Do you work on docks?+

Yes. Dock electrical installation, wiring and maintenance are part of Flagg Electric's services. The project gallery includes dock work in Sunrise Beach and Rocky Mount.

What does the work cost?+

Every property and every job is different, so estimates are given per project. Call with a description of the work and you'll get the scope and the estimate process directly.

What should I have ready when I call?+

Your location, a short description of the work or problem, and your preferred timing are a useful starting point. Ask about scheduling, the scope of work and the estimate process directly.

How do I request service?+

Call (573) 286-3406 to discuss your project and availability. You can also find Flagg Electric on Facebook.
